# Counterpoint privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Counterpoint has no server and no account. The developer never receives your data.

## What Counterpoint reads
- The text of a web page, only when you ask: by clicking the Counterpoint button, pressing the shortcut, choosing "Counterpoint this" on selected text, or clicking "Counterpoint this page" in the panel.
- If you turn on "Run on its own when I switch pages" and give Chrome's permission to read pages, it reads the page you switch to so it can answer without a click.

## Where page text goes
- **Chrome built-in AI:** the text stays on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Claude, OpenAI, or Gemini:** the text is sent directly from your browser to the provider you picked, using your own API key, and nowhere else. That provider's privacy policy applies to what you send them.

## What Counterpoint stores
- Your settings and API keys, in your browser's local extension storage. They never leave your browser except for the key going to its own provider.
- Recent answers, in session storage, so reopening the panel doesn't re-run a page. They're cleared when you close Chrome.

## What Counterpoint never does
- No analytics, tracking, ads, or selling of data.
- No browsing history. Beyond the recent answers above, which stay on your computer, it keeps no record of the pages you visit.
