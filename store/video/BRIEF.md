## Intent

A calm, Google-style promo for Counterpoint, an open-source Chrome extension. No voiceover,
silent: on-screen headlines carry the story with sound off. Story: a product page and the line
"Every page wants you to believe something." → the side panel slides in with the short answer
(The page says / The other side / three arguments) → Say more adds a comparison table and
What would settle it → a follow-up question and answer → highlight a claim, right-click,
Counterpoint this → end card with the logo mark, name, tagline, "Free on Chrome's built-in AI ·
Open source" and github.com/n8peace/counterpoint.
